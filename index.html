Honest about what it does

No “100% undetectable” promises. Just a better draft that you stay in control of.

It will

  • Rephrase repetitive, formulaic sentences in the tone you choose
  • Keep numbers, names, links and citations, and warn you if any changed
  • Show a word-by-word comparison with your original
  • Work without an account, with history kept only in your browser

It won’t

  • Guarantee any result from Turnitin, GPTZero or other AI detectors
  • Check whether your facts are correct, which is still your job
  • Make it acceptable to submit AI-written work where it isn’t allowed
  • Replace your own ideas, sources or voice

Frequently asked questions

Still curious? See the full FAQ.

What does an AI humanizer actually do?

It rewrites text to fix the habits that make AI drafts feel mechanical: sentences of the same length, piled-up transition words, vague buzzwords and over-formal phrasing. The goal is writing that reads naturally to a person. It works like a fast editing pass, not a way to create new content.

Will the rewritten text pass AI detectors?

We can’t promise that, and no honest tool can. AI detectors are statistical, they disagree with each other, and they change often. They also flag human writing by mistake. Text Humanize is built to improve readability, not to beat a particular detector. If your school or workplace asks you to disclose AI use, disclose it.

Is my text stored or shared?

Your text is sent over an encrypted connection to our server and to our AI provider to create the rewrite. We don’t sell it, publish it or use it to build profiles. To speed up repeat requests, an identical request’s result may be cached for up to one hour and then deleted. Your history stays in your own browser. Full details are in our privacy policy.

Is it free? Are there limits?

Yes, it’s free and supported by advertising. Each request can be up to 10,000 characters (roughly 1,500 words), and there’s a short per-minute limit to keep the service fast for everyone. For longer documents, work through them section by section.

Can students use Text Humanize?

Yes, within the rules of your course. Many schools allow AI for brainstorming or editing but not for writing graded work. Check your syllabus, ask your instructor when in doubt, and never submit work as your own when that breaks your institution’s policy. Our responsible use policy explains more.

Which languages does it support?

It’s designed for and tested with English. It may handle other languages, but the results are less reliable, so review non-English output carefully.
