What cookies are

Cookies are small files a website stores in your browser. Similar technologies, such as local storage, work in a comparable way. Some are needed for a site to work; others help measure usage or show advertising.

Your choices

When you first visit, a consent message lets you accept or decline non-essential cookies. You can change your mind at any time using in the footer of every page. Some changes, such as switching ad personalisation, take effect the next time a page loads.

For visitors in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, analytics and advertising storage is off by default until you accept. In these regions the consent message may be provided through Google’s certified consent management platform, which stores your choice in its own cookies. If you decline, Google may still show non-personalised ads, which use limited cookies for purposes such as frequency capping and fraud prevention.

Other ways to control cookies

  • Browser settings: most browsers let you block or delete cookies and site data.
  • Google ad personalisation: manage it at adssettings.google.com.
  • Industry opt-outs: optout.aboutads.info (US) and youronlinechoices.eu (EU).
  • Google Analytics: the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on.
