Get in touch

Questions, bug reports, privacy requests or corrections: email is the fastest way to reach us.

Last updated September 13, 2026

Email us

hello@text-humanize.com
We aim to reply within two business days.

What to include

Reporting a bug

  • What you were trying to do and what happened instead (an error message or screenshot helps)
  • Your browser and device, for example “Chrome on Android”
  • The settings you used (tone, amount of change, writing type)

Please don’t send confidential or personal text. A short, non-sensitive sample that reproduces the problem is enough.

Privacy and data requests

To ask what personal data we hold about you, or to have it corrected or deleted, email us with the subject line “Privacy request”. Tell us which email address or details the request relates to. Our privacy policy explains your rights.

Corrections to our guides

If something in one of our guides is wrong or out of date, send the page link and, if possible, a source. We review every correction request under our editorial policy.

Reporting misuse

If you believe the service is being used in breach of our responsible use policy, let us know what you’ve seen.

Partnerships and advertising

We don’t sell sponsored articles or paid rankings. For other business enquiries, email us with a short description of your proposal.

Before you write

Many common questions, such as limits, supported languages and how your text is handled, are answered in our FAQ.

Who you’re contacting

Text Humanize is an independent website. It’s run by a small team, not a large company, so your message goes straight to the people who build the tool and write the guides.
