Why Originality.ai Flags Almost Everything

Originality.ai is widely recognized in the SEO and digital marketing community as one of the most sensitive AI detectors on the market. In its quest to prevent AI spam, its models (including Originality 3.0 Standard and Lite) frequently produce aggressive false positives on completely human writing that exhibits clean, professional phrasing.

Originality.ai inspects content using deep transformer-based classifiers that look for:

  • Token Predictability: If sentences follow conventional syntax with high mathematical probability, Originality assigns an elevated AI probability.
  • Cliché AI Vocabulary: Overused LLM transition words (such as 'tapestry', 'delve', 'moreover', 'testament') immediately drive AI scores above 80%.
  • Uniform Sentence Lengths: Paragraphs where every sentence contains between 15 and 20 words are heavily penalized.

The Problem with Paid Bypass Tools

When writers see an '89% AI Detected' badge on Originality.ai, many panic and purchase expensive subscriptions to tools like Undetectable.ai or HIX Bypass. However, these tools often charge up to $20/month, lock basic features behind strict word caps, and frequently scramble the text so badly that it requires extensive manual editing.
