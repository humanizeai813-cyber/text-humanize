AI-generated output

The humanizer uses AI language models to rewrite text. Automated rewrites can introduce mistakes, drop nuance, or subtly change meaning, even with the safeguards we use. Always read the output carefully, check facts, figures and quotations against your sources, and edit the text before you use it. You are responsible for anything you publish or submit.

No guarantee about AI detectors

We don’t claim, and can’t guarantee, that rewritten text will pass or fail any AI-detection tool. Detection tools use different methods, are updated frequently and frequently disagree. Results you see today may differ tomorrow.

Our AI detector is an estimate

The score from our AI detector reflects measurable writing patterns and, where enabled, an external model’s estimate. It can be wrong in both directions. It must not be used as the only evidence that a person did or didn’t use AI, or as the basis for academic, disciplinary or employment decisions.

Educational content, not professional advice

Our guides are general information about writing and AI tools. They aren’t legal, academic or professional advice. Rules on AI use differ between schools, courses, employers and publishers; always follow the policies that apply to you and ask the relevant person if you’re unsure.

Accuracy and currency

We work to keep our content accurate and up to date and follow a published editorial policy. AI tools change quickly, though, so some details, such as product features or policies, may become outdated. If you notice an error, please tell us.

External links
